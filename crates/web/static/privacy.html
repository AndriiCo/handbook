3. How we share data

We share personal data only to provide the Service, and only what each recipient needs:

  • Login providers (Google, Apple, Meta): the standard authentication handshake when you choose that sign-in method.
  • Shipping carriers (USPS, FedEx): names, addresses, and package details for labels you ask us to buy or print.
  • Telephony provider (Telnyx): staff-initiated verification calls to public offices; your personal data is not part of those calls.
  • Legal compliance: where disclosure is required by law.

We do not sell personal data. We do not share it with data brokers.

Stored secrets are encrypted with keys held outside the database; staff access requires passkeys plus mutual-TLS client certificates; administrative changes are step-up authenticated and audited. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify affected users of breaches as the law requires.
