Operating-system distribution images

A container base image (Debian, Ubuntu, and the official images built on
them) is a collection of separately licensed packages, not one work under
one licence. The image's own build recipe carries the licence named in the
table; every package inside the image keeps its own licence.

Each package's copyright notices and licence texts ship inside the image
itself, under /usr/share/doc/<package>/copyright, and in the distribution's
source archive:

  Debian: https://www.debian.org/legal/licenses/
          https://sources.debian.org/
  Ubuntu: https://ubuntu.com/legal/intellectual-property-policy
          https://launchpad.net/ubuntu/+source/

Packages the Apostille Handbook installs on top of a base image are listed
in the table individually, with their own licences.
