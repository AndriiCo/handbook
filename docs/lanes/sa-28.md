## Goal
The customer flow: /order picks destination, document, form and one of
four delivery options with live quotes and cited disabled reasons, signs
in, pays, and the cabinet shows every order moving live.

## You own (touch nothing else)
- `web/src/routes/(customer)/{order,orders,billing,shipping}/**`
- `web/src/lib/customer/**`
- `web/e2e/fixtures/orders-*.json`, `web/e2e/commerce.spec.mjs` (new,
  non-gating)

## Steps, in order (estimate; train)
1. **W3a** (6h; T2): /order: Destination → Document (from `plan.paths`,
   falling back to accepted fact topics) → Form → Delivery (4 cards from
   `order-options.json`: FedEx labels, USPS labels, mailing kit, free
   address label; disabled cards cite their fact; sign-in gate here) →
   Pay (Checkout redirect; 503 `paid_tiers_paused` state). Keep the
   stepper's `aria-current="step"` (GP4 scoreboard row).
2. **W3b** (6h; T2/T3): /orders (cards from `orders-list.json`),
   /orders/[id] (timeline from `events`, legs with tracking, label PDFs,
   receipt) live on `billing:{customer_ref}` and `shipment:{id}` (no
   polling); /billing real receipts; /shipping drops the hardcoded
   `authorityId='1'` and staff controls.
